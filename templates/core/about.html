About Us

Our History

Kabuku Secondary School is an Advanced Level institution located in Kabuku, Handeni District, Tanga Region, Tanzania. The school has grown to serve over 2,000 students, providing quality education in science and mathematics combinations.

Our commitment to academic excellence and character development has made us a respected institution in the region.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence that produces competent graduates who contribute to national development and global progress.

Mission

To provide quality Advanced Level education through dedicated teaching, modern learning approaches, and a supportive environment that nurtures intellectual growth, discipline, and social responsibility.

Headmaster's Welcome Message

Mwezi Mchanga

Headmaster

Welcome to Kabuku Secondary School. As an institution dedicated to "Education for Development," we strive to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to excel in higher education and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our PGM and PCM combinations provide a strong foundation in sciences and mathematics, opening doors to engineering, medicine, technology, and environmental sciences. We are proud of our growing community of over 2,000 students and our dedicated staff.

I invite you to explore our website and discover what makes Kabuku Secondary School a place of learning, growth, and opportunity.
