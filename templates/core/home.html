Welcome to Kabuku Secondary School

A centre of academic excellence located in Kabuku, Handeni - Tanga, Tanzania.

Under the leadership of Headmaster Mwezi Mchanga, our school is committed to providing quality Advanced Level education that prepares students for higher learning and national development.

With over 2,000 students, we offer a vibrant learning environment focused on science and mathematics through our PGM and PCM combinations.
